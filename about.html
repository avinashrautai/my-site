About

A little about who I am, what I build and why this website exists.

Hello.

I'm Avinash. I spend a lot of time observing the web, software, people and the small details that most people walk past without noticing. Many of the ideas on this website start from simple observations rather than grand plans.

I am a hobbyist coder, blogger and builder of small digital projects. I enjoy exploring publishing, writing tools, note-taking systems, design decisions and the way technology influences everyday work.

Over the years I have been fascinated by how ideas move from rough notes to finished products. Sometimes that product is a website. Sometimes it becomes software. Sometimes it becomes an article or a book.

This website serves as a place to collect those experiments, thoughts and observations. It is part notebook, part archive and part workshop.

Most of the projects here are intentionally simple. I prefer tools that solve real problems over tools that simply add more features.

When I am not writing or building, I am usually researching, sketching ideas, reading discussions, exploring old software, studying design systems or looking for patterns hidden inside everyday experiences.

If something here resonates with you, feel free to explore the writing section or take a look at the projects.
